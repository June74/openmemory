# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenMemory contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive for people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Explain unfamiliar technical language when it affects participation.
- Accept evidence-based correction and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect private information and credentials encountered during collaboration.
- Respect project scope, review gates, and maintainers' responsibility for user safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, repeated disruption, or knowingly unsafe contributions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method published in the GitHub security policy. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the OpenMemory project.
